IMAGES FOR CLAVYX
=================
Drop your generated images in this folder using the exact file names below and
they will appear automatically. If an image is missing, the page shows a tasteful
stone-coloured placeholder instead, so nothing ever breaks.

Recommended art direction for every image: fine pen and ink engraving style,
warm parchment background, deep ink linework with a single soft brass/gold
highlight. Architectural, scholarly, timeless. No text in the image.

1) hero.png            (about 1400x1000)
   Prompt: A fine pen and ink engraving of a lone classical stone archway on warm
   parchment, its keystone glowing softly in brass gold while the rest is quiet
   ink linework, subtle cross hatching, scholarly and timeless, no text.

   Prompt: An engraving style illustration of an arch drawn as a diagram, faint

   keyhole cut into its centre glowing brass, warm parchment background, no text.

   Prompt: An engraving of an arch under gentle pressure lines, one stone

   no text.
