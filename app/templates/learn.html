Field notes

Think better on purpose

A short guide to the ideas CLAVYX is built on: assumptions, keystones, and the fair opposing case.

What is a hidden assumption?

It is a belief your argument needs to be true but never states. Naming it is the first step to testing it.

What is a keystone?

The one assumption that carries the arch. Remove it and the conclusion collapses. Focus your effort here.

What is a steelman?

The strongest fair version of the opposing view. Beating a steelman means your idea is genuinely strong.

What is myside bias?

The habit of judging arguments more kindly when they support what we already believe. CLAVYX counters it.
