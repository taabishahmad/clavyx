Under the hood

How CLAVYX works

Seven small, checkable steps turn a messy argument into a clear picture of what holds it up and where it could break.

1

Decomposition

Your text is split into the main conclusion and the reasons you actually stated.

2

Assumption excavation

The unstated premises the argument silently depends on are surfaced.

3

Minimal sufficient set

A greedy search removes assumptions one by one, keeping only those the conclusion cannot do without. These are your keystones.

4

Influence ordering

Assumptions are ranked from most to least load bearing, shown as an ordering rather than a false precise number.

5

The opposing case

A genuine steelman, aimed at your keystone, never a strawman.

6

Revision evidence

The concrete proof that would rationally change your conclusion, tied to the assumption it tests.

7

Your report

Everything is assembled into a map, a keystone view, a stress test, and an evidence ledger.

Honest by design

The support figure in the stress test is an estimate, shown to make dependency tangible. It is never presented as a measured probability. Where a model grades an output, its grades are first checked against human judgement.
